4.10. Corrections relative to the lecture slides#

Corrections to the lecture slides

The following changes were made relative to the Hidden information: adverse selection slides and the Adverse selection: two cases slides. Slide numbers refer to those decks.

  1. Theorem 1 (slides 25–30). The theorem holds only for \(1<k<2\). For \(k\ge2\), buyers’ value of the average unit is at least the best seller’s outside option, every unit trades at \(k/2\), and the outcome is efficient (Proposition 4.3). The proof on slide 29 divides by \(p^*\) without treating \(p^*=0\) separately and never compares prices above \(1\).

  2. Slide 25. “Every quality \(\theta\) of the product is sold at a unique price \(p^*(\theta)=0\)” should read: the only price is \(0\), and only quality \(\theta=0\) is sold.

  3. Slides 17 and 23. The full extraction of surplus by sellers is not “third-degree price discrimination”. It follows from buyers being on the long side of the market; different qualities are different goods.

  4. Slide 23. The take-aways of Proposition 2 are copied from Proposition 1: under Proposition 2 the market is not fragmented into sub-markets. The welfare expression is the ex post surplus from a unit of quality \(\theta\), not the welfare of a sub-market.

  5. Slide 21. “By an argument analogous to the proof of Proposition 2” should refer to Proposition 1.

  6. Slides 38–39. The spiral is drawn with buyers paying \(p/2\), \(p/4\), …, which corresponds to \(k=1\). With \(k>1\) buyers pay \(kp/2\), so the price falls by the factor \(k/2\) each round (Proposition 4.4).

  7. Lemma 1 (slides 42–45). Buyers’ value of the average unit is \(k\E[\theta]=k/2\), not \(1/2\). The correct statement is: every unit trades at \(k/2\) if \(r\le k/2\), and nothing trades if \(r>k/2\) (Proposition 4.5). “Efficient” should read “efficient given what can be observed”: ex post, units with \(k\theta<r\) are traded although sellers value them more.

  8. Slide 32. The idea that prices aggregate information is due to Hayek (1945); Schumpeter is not the right attribution.

  9. Placeholders. “(see Slide xx)” on slides 4 and 41, the blank “Preview of results” and “Broader literature” slides, and the generic “Motivation” slides on optimisation are unfinished. The “Our plan for this section” slide contains text from the signalling deck (“when the worker only knows \(\theta\) … (Lemma ??)”).

  10. Typos. “Porpositions” (slide 24), “both gets o.o” (slide 5), “Predictions of Theorem 1 does not depend” (slide 31). The author’s name is spelled “Chakrbarti” on the title slides.

  11. Two-cases deck, slide 12. The formula \(p^*=ka/(2-k)\) applies while it is below \(1\); for \(a\ge(2-k)/k\) every certified unit trades at \(k(a+1)/2\) (Proposition 4.6).
