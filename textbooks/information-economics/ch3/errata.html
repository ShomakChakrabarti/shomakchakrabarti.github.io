3.9. Corrections relative to the lecture slides#

Corrections to the lecture slides

The following changes were made relative to the Economics of hidden actions slides. Slide numbers refer to that deck.

  1. Main example. The slides use Safelite Auto Glass as the main example. This book uses NHS family practices instead (Example 3.1 and Example 3.2). The running example keeps the same numbers, now read as thousands of pounds a year, so every calculation carries over.

  2. Slide 7. The probability ordering is printed as “\(\mu>\bar\mu\)”; it should read \(\muh>\mul\).

  3. Slide 8. The principal extracts all surplus, which is first-degree (perfect) price discrimination, not third-degree.

  4. Slides 3 and later. Pronouns for principal and agent switch; standardised here (principal she, agent he).

  5. Slide 13. Missing bracket in the principal’s expected payoff; written out in full here.

  6. Slides 31 and 54. The low-action profit is written with \(\muh\) instead of \(\mul\). With \(\muh\) in both lines the output terms cancel; the stated conditions (3.1) and (3.4) are correct only with \(\mul\) in the low-action line.

  7. Slide 34. The certainty equivalent is denoted \(c\), which clashes with the cost of effort. Not used here.

  8. Slides 36 and 59 (section openers). Placeholder text from the signalling deck (“We first study the market outcomes … \(\theta\)”) appears under “Our plan for this section”.

  9. Slide 37. The sentence “A contract in this situation is a…” is unfinished. A contract under hidden action is a pair \((w_g,w_b)\) that cannot depend on \(a\).

  10. Slide 41. The condition for recommending the high action is truncated (“iff l\(\Delta\)”). The complete condition is (3.4).

  11. Slide 56. The weight on the bad-state utility should be \(1-\muh\), not \(\mul\).

  12. Slides 59, 62 and 64. The bad-state utility is written \(\uo+\mul c/\Dpi\); it should be \(\uo-\mul c/\Dpi\), as in Theorem 3.2.

  13. Slide 60. In \(S^{\FB}(a)\) the bad-state term uses \(y_g\); it should be \(y_b\).

  14. Slide 62. Titled “first best” but computes second-best welfare.

  15. Slide 65. The definitions of \(\bar w_b\) and \(\bar w_g\) are swapped: \(\bar w_g=h(\uo+(1-\mul)c/\Dpi)\) and \(\bar w_b=h(\uo-\mul c/\Dpi)\).

  16. Implicit assumption. The second-best contract requires \(\uo-\mul c/\Dpi\) to lie in the range of \(u\) (Assumption 3.3); otherwise limited liability binds.
